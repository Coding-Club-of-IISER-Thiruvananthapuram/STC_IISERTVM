About the STC

Where Questions Lead, and Curiosity is Celebrated.

Our Mission

The Science and Technology Council (STC) at IISER Thiruvananthapuram is committed to promoting scientific inquiry, technological advancement, and interdisciplinary research within the institute. Its mission is to cultivate an intellectually stimulating environment where students and researchers are encouraged to explore innovative solutions to complex problems. By organizing academic seminars, workshops, outreach programs, and collaborative projects, the STC seeks to enhance scientific thinking, foster creativity, and build leadership skills among its members. The council also emphasizes the importance of responsible and ethical research, aiming to bridge the gap between scientific knowledge and a student's understanding of research.

Our History

The STC was established to serve as a central platform that connects the diverse academic interests of students and faculty at IISER TVM. Founded by a group of enthusiastic students, in response to a growing interest in scientific clubs and platforms for discussion, the council began as a forum to exchange ideas and support research initiatives across various scientific disciplines. Over the years, it has expanded its activities to include seminars, talks, exhibitions, school outreaches, and public engagement efforts. Through its continued dedication to excellence in science and technology, the STC plays a vital role in shaping the research culture of IISER TVM, inspiring innovation, and nurturing the next generation of researchers.

Our Structure

The Science and Technology Council (STC) is a constituent body of the Students’ Affairs Council (SAC) of IISER Thiruvananthapuram. STC is led by a Secretary, supported by council members who serve as representatives of the student community. Together, they work to promote scientific awareness, collaboration, and communication across the campus. The council functions under the guidance of the Faculties-in-Charge (FIC) and the Dean of Student Affairs (DoSA). STC oversees several affiliated clubs, each dedicated to a particular domain of science or technology. These clubs together with council actively organize events, discussions, and hands-on activities, and are responsible for planning and conducting Anvesha, the annual science fest of IISER Thiruvananthapuram.

Accessing Crucible is a two-step process, depending on your need.

1. For General Access to the Workspace:
The main Crucible area is an open space for all students to collaborate, ideate, and work on projects. The key can be collected from the Medical Center reception desk to access this common area.

2. For Access to Specialized Labs & Equipment:
To use the specialized labs (e.g., physics lab, chemistry lab), you must first receive approval from the SnT Council. Please follow this procedure:
Step 1: Draft an email explaining the project you wish to work on. Be sure to mention the specific lab or equipment you require and the purpose of your work (e.g., course project, club activity, personal innovation).
Step 2: Email the SnT Council at stc@iisertvm.ac.in.
Step 3: The council will review your request. Once approved, you will be notified and granted access to the specific lab resources.

Please note: Access to specific equipment may require a brief safety and training orientation.

Anvesha is the annual tech-fest that happens in the Varsha semester. It is a grand stage where we invite the brightest minds to showcase groundbreaking projects, learn from research visionaries through talks and workshops, and compete in events pushing science's boundaries. It is a place for everyone to explore their passion, gain new skills, and connect with like-minded peers. To begin your quest, explore the Anvesha Section to find out more.

Anvesha unfolds over two main periods. A series of build-up events, workshops, and preliminary rounds spans approximately one month. This all leads to the main attraction: a concentrated and high-energy 3-day festival. To find out the specific dates for this year, please check the official Anvesha schedule on our website.
